How to read these numbers

This governor board applies the governor-only same-state historical polling-error method to a dated, attributed On Point Politics polling average as the current polling input. The historical polling-error calculation uses only earlier governor races from the same state, never Senate or presidential polling errors. Ervin’s Take also uses a small Ohio candidate-performance shift, documented with result links in the state ledger. Poll counts reflect the publisher’s records, not distinct independent forecasters. Candidate rosters are cross-referenced with the National Governors Association. No county result is treated as live until a verified source has provided it. The full historical validation data and individual polling/result sources remain available in the research archive.
