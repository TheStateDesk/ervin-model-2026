REPRODUCIBILITY & RISK

How this works — and what it cannot know

14,000 new draws

A seeded exploratory scenario ensemble varies national, regional, suburban/rural and race-specific Democratic margin shocks. It is not the missing historic 14,000-run archive and is not fitted to precinct reporting-time data.

Conditional reweighting

Early race outcomes are compared with their Ervin baseline. Draws consistent with an observed departure receive greater weight, propagating common swing assumptions toward TX, KS and IA. This is a scenario sensitivity, not empirically calibrated winning odds.

Compositional guard

Automatic use requires a dated, matched source, adequate turnout share, multiple precinct reporting units, documented geographic coverage, and audited absentee/early versus in-person composition. A precincts-reporting percentage alone fails the gate.

Correlations are provisional

Virginia suburbs, rural Kentucky and Ohio may signal different national coalition components. Correlations and estimated ranges here are explicit judgmental priors; do not treat them as measured cross-state predictive accuracy.
