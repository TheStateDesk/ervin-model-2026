Historical forecast error and uncertainty — underlying evidence

From archived historical outcomes, not simulated votes: 61 Senate elections in 2018–2024 had a 4.09-point average absolute polling-margin error and a 9.28-point 90th-percentile absolute error. A separate comparison of 232 geometrically comparable House districts between 2022 and 2024 had 4.22-point mean absolute error and 8.35-point 90th-percentile absolute error. The House sample excluded 203 noncomparable districts; the historical House benchmark is not a validation of the full Ervin 2026 model.

These are errors in the Democratic-minus-Republican margin, not sampling margins of error, confidence intervals, race calls or indications that 90% of future races will fall within these bounds.
