The Ervin editorial standard

Every state gets geographic coverage even where there is no modeled Senate race or gubernatorial election. Candidate ratings and publisher averages are kept separate from independently calculated models, historical county returns, and actual election results.

What is not inferred

The existence of a House district on a map does not certify the applicable 2026 boundary. A county scenario is not independently collected county polling. No nationwide real-time turnout estimate, calibrated House win probability, or unverified race call is silently manufactured.
