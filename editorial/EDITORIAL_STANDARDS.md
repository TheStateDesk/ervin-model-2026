# The State Desk — Editorial Standards and Perspective

Status: Proposed newsroom policy; requires Gabriel Ervin's editorial approval before publication.

## Editorial perspective
The State Desk approaches American politics from a **compassionate, socially conservative and economically progressive** perspective, without alignment with democratic socialism or the Democratic Socialists of America.

- **Social and cultural questions:** Treat Christianity, religious institutions, family stability, local communities and duties to neighbors as serious elements of public life. Respect human dignity and cover contested issues fairly. When relevant, examine evidence-based proposals for reducing abortion rates and unintended pregnancies, without adopting an explicit editorial stance against legal abortion or same-sex marriage.
- **Economics:** Favor serious examination of organized labor, family-support policies, affordable health care, antitrust enforcement, domestic manufacturing, wage growth and economic security. Apply critical scrutiny to concentrated corporate power, while acknowledging markets, private enterprise and fiscal tradeoffs. Do not characterize this as socialism or affiliate the publication with the DSA.
- **Electoral institutions:** Favor evaluating a larger U.S. House of Representatives and the case for more proportionate representation, while opposing abolition of the Senate or Electoral College as an editorial preference. Report accurately on competing reform proposals, including arguments and evidence against the paper's preferences.
- **Political independence:** Criticize and credit officials of either party on the merits; do not manipulate election results, polling, source selection or facts to suit the editorial perspective.

## Newsroom safeguards
1. **News is not advocacy:** Report verified developments without altering facts to fit the editorial viewpoint. Mark analysis, opinion, forecasts and reporting distinctly.
2. **Sourcing:** Link primary records whenever possible; credit original newsrooms for significant reported facts, distinguish official records from reported accounts, and independently verify sensitive claims.
3. **Numbers:** Attach a claim manifest to each proposed edition, with precise source URLs/files and publication/update times.
4. **Transparency:** Display a permanent AI-assistance notice in the homepage footer. Keep detailed sourcing and corrections accessible; do not suggest AI-generated summaries are original interviews or firsthand reporting.
5. **Corrections:** Date corrections and document what changed. Preserve archived election forecasts and historical reporting.
6. **Publication:** Editorial automation may prepare draft branches and pull requests only. Only explicit approval by Gabriel Ervin authorizes merge.

This document defines editorial priorities, not a license to omit contrary evidence or publish unsupported allegations.
