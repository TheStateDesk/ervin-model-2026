About The State Desk

The State Desk is an independent publication covering Congress, the White House, the states and elections. It is edited and published by Gabriel Ervin and publishes the Ervin forecast.

What we cover

We examine polling, elections, political institutions, candidates and American public affairs. Our special interest is original analysis of archived election data: frozen forecasts, historical comparisons, state results and county-level changes. Where an article summarizes reporting from another newsroom, we name and link that source rather than claiming to have conducted its interviews.

Our working process

Our newsroom draws on public records, source-linked data and published reporting from other outlets. Our sources may include newsrooms, government election offices, polling organizations and public documents. Every quotation is attributed to its source, and each story identifies whether its reporting draws on public records, data or other news organizations.

Election results

Race calls come from the AP and other decision desks, credited in each story. Vote counts are unofficial until certified. The Nov. 3, 2026 forecast is archived as published.

Corrections and contact

Send factual corrections with the affected page, exact passage and supporting citation to our public issue tracker. Substantive changes are logged on the affected article and the Corrections page.
