THE PUBLICATION

Independent political data analysis.

The State Desk is the public newsroom of the Ervin Model, published by Gabriel Ervin.

What we cover

We examine polling, elections, political institutions, candidates and American public affairs. Our special interest is original analysis of archived election data: frozen forecasts, historical comparisons, state results and county-level changes. Where an article summarizes reporting from another newsroom, we name and link that source rather than claiming to have conducted its interviews.

Our working process

We use AI assistance for drafts, programming, numerical analysis and editorial organization. AI assistance is disclosed on articles. Our sources may include newsrooms, government election offices, polling organizations and public documents. Quotations must be real and attributed; no interview, eyewitness observation or human review is claimed unless it happened.

Election results

We do not operate an independent race-calling desk. Election calls are attributed to professional decision desks. Historical vote snapshots should be treated as dated, unofficial observations, not final canvasses or a licensed real-time AP feed. We preserve the Nov. 3, 2026 forecast as published, even where it was wrong.

Corrections and contact

Send factual corrections with the affected page, exact passage and supporting citation to our public issue tracker. Substantive changes are logged on the affected article and the Corrections page.
