How we report the election

Results reporting and analysis are separate. Our election-night calls and dated November 4 vote snapshots credit the original AP-sourced tabulations; they are neither live API access nor certified final returns. The interpretive reporting in these features draws independently on regional reporters, Reuters, Axios, CBS News, candidate policy documents, certified Federal Election Commission records and our frozen November 2 polling archive. We link claims to the outlet that supplied the information.
