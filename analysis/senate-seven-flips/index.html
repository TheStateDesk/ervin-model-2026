THE SENATE / SEVEN FLIPS

Seven states that changed the Senate

Democrats won seven Senate seats previously held by Republicans, including victories in Kansas, South Carolina and Texas.

THE ERVIN MODEL / DATA DESK • NOVEMBER 4, 2026 • 3 MIN READ

The Senate changed hands not through a single swing-state upset, but through a cluster of wins across traditionally Republican and competitive states. Associated Press reporting confirms Democratic victories in Iowa, Kansas, Maine, North Carolina, Ohio, South Carolina and Texas.

Kansas had not elected a Democratic U.S. senator since 1932, according to Ballotpedia's November 4 review. Iowa had not elected one since 2008. Those two results illustrate how far beyond the usual set of battlegrounds this midterm election reached.
