THE FINAL COUNT / CLOSE RACES

Two Senate contests still demand attention

Alaska and Mississippi remained exceptionally close as the count continued into November 4.

THE ERVIN MODEL / DATA DESK • NOVEMBER 4, 2026 • 3 MIN READ

By Wednesday, the 2026 Senate majority had been decided, but some individual outcomes were still unresolved. NBC News and Associated Press reporting identified Alaska and Mississippi among the contests remaining too close to call.

In such races, the margin in reported votes can be dramatically smaller than the number of ballots still being processed. A temporary lead should not be confused with a final result, and Alaska's multi-candidate contest requires particular care when comparing candidate vote totals.

The latest source-attributed totals below are not substitutes for election-authority certification. Because feeds may arrive at different times, always compare their report timestamps before drawing conclusions about a change in the margin.
