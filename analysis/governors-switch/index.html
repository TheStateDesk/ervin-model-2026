STATEHOUSES / FOUR FLIPS

Four governorships turn Democratic

Georgia, Iowa, Ohio and Texas elected Democratic governors, changing the partisan control of four state executives.

THE ERVIN MODEL / DATA DESK • NOVEMBER 4, 2026 • 3 MIN READ

The national result reached beyond Washington. Democrats won the governorships of Georgia, Iowa, Ohio and Texas, displacing Republican control in each of those states, according to the Associated Press.

That shift changes state-level governing power in places that had been led by Republican governors. It also complicates the national story: state elections can have distinct candidates and political dynamics, even during an unusually strong congressional election for one party.
