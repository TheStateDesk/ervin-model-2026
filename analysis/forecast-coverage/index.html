How this is scored

House: Compare the positive/negative Democratic-minus-Republican projected margin for all 435 House districts with D/R parties on individually documented calls. Because the recorded call identifies a party rather than a pairwise vote-margin, this is a party-direction check, not candidate-level or margin accuracy. Same-party candidate contests need separate candidate-level review.

Senate: Compare the frozen adjusted statewide D–R margin to the called party in only the modeled states. Any independent-candidate contest requires candidate-specific treatment and is excluded from D/R agreement until validated. The count excludes other contests that had no comparable frozen model.

Governors: The frozen governor data contains the underlying public polling margins and independent historical corrections, not a simple comparable governor forecast field. This preliminary board withholds gubernatorial scoring until the complete frozen adjustment is reproduced and the relevant outcomes are documented.

Not measured yet: A nationally complete error score, certified vote margins, and probabilistic calibration/Brier scores. The current call ledger does not contain final D–R margins for all races, and early calls disproportionately cover decisive contests. A high House agreement rate here should not be interpreted as a reliable national model score.
