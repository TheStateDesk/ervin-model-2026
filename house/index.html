Coverage and methodology

All 435 House districts are indexed from U.S. state apportionment. Individual ratings are only displayed for entries transcribed from Cook Political Report's October 30, 2026 assessment, with a cross-outlet comparison. The Ervin House scenario separately provides provisional numerical margins for all districts, based on historical returns, the national generic ballot, and qualifying surveys. Published Cook ratings remain an independent editorial benchmark. Incumbent names are historical context, not a verified 2026 ballot slate. Redistricting can change district boundaries and invalidate comparisons with old returns.
